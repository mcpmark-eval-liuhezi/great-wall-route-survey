## Directions

**Leg 1 - Start to Badaling** (75.6 km, 1 hour 15 mins, via Jingzang Expy/G6): head north on Zhengyi Road, turn right onto East Chang'an Avenue and continue on Jianguomennei Dajie, then turn left onto the 2nd Ring Road northbound for 8.7 km. Exit at Deshengmen East Street, follow the ramp and Dewai Street to merge onto the Jingzang Expressway (G6 / Badaling Expressway, toll road) for 58.8 km. Take the exit toward Badaling Road/S325 and turn right, following Badaling Road for 2.4 km to the wall.

**Leg 2 - Badaling to Summer Palace** (67.6 km, 1 hour 5 mins, via Jingzang Expy/G6): head northwest from the wall and rejoin Badaling Road/S325 for 2.6 km, then at the roundabout take the 3rd exit onto the G6 ramp toward Beijing Downtown and merge onto the Jingzang Expressway (G6, toll road) southbound for 35.3 km. Keep left to stay on G6/G110 for 19.6 km following signs for the North 5th Ring and Shangqing Bridge, take the exit toward the North 5th Ring/Yuanmingyuan/G7 and merge onto the 5th Ring (S50) for 4.5 km. Exit toward Yiheyuan/Yuanmingyuan, turn right onto Yuanmingyuan West Road, then right onto Yiheyuan Road and finally a short left turn to arrive at the Summer Palace.

**Leg 3 - Summer Palace back to start** (25.0 km, 40 mins, via the 4th Ring): head north and turn right onto Yiheyuan Road, then right onto Gongmenqian Street and Kunminghu East Road to reach the ramp for the 4th Ring Road. Merge onto the West 4th Ring and continue 5.5 km, then keep left to stay on the 4th Ring for another 3.1 km following signs for Wukesong Bridge. Take the exit toward Lianhuachi West Road / West 3rd Ring, continue on Lianhuachi West Road then Lianhuachi East Road (4.1 km) into the city centre, and continue along Xuanwumen West Street and Xuanwumen East Street to Qianmen West and East Streets. Finally, turn left onto Zhengyi Road, where the destination is on the right.

**Loop summary:** 168.2 km and approximately 3 hours of driving in total. The route climbs from 45.3 m at the downtown start to 768.1 m at Badaling, then descends to 52.4 m at the Summer Palace before returning to downtown at 45.3 m. The G6 toll road carries most of the mileage.
